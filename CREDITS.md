Six Lanterns is a private hobby game by Nir and Orel. It is not sold or distributed, but the repository is public, so every third-party asset that ships with the game is listed here with its license. If you are the author of something listed and want it credited differently or removed, open an issue.

Only sources whose files are actually in this repository are listed as "in use". Last audit: 8 November 2026 (`node tools/fx-audit.js` for the effects library).

## In use

### Mochi Lab: effect sprite sheets

- **What:** every sheet in `fx/*.webp` (256 sheets, listed in `tools/fx-list.json`), used for skills, hits, weather and UI effects through `js/fxlib.js`. Also the contact sheets in `docs/refs/fx-catalog/` (one small middle frame per sheet, for browsing the catalog).
- **Source:** Mochi Lab "Mega Bundle" (43 packs), purchased on itch.io: https://mochilab-studio.itch.io/
- **License:** commercial asset license from the itch.io purchase. Redistributed only as the converted, downscaled sheets the game actually uses; the original packs (17 GB) are not in the repository. **To verify against the product page:** the exact license text was not saved in the repository, so the terms above are a summary. If the product license forbids redistribution of modified sheets in a public repository, the sheets must move out of the public repo.

### game-icons.net: skill, tree, weapon and menu icons

- **What:** the SVG paths embedded in `js/gicons-data.js`, drawn by `js/gicons.js`. Colors and frames were changed.
- **Authors:** Lorc, Delapouite, Sbed, Skoll and other contributors. The exact per-icon list is generated by `tools/gicons.js` into [docs/credits.md](docs/credits.md); do not edit that list by hand.
- **License:** [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Requires attribution (given in docs/credits.md, in the in-game Credits window and in the settings window) and a note that the icons were modified.
- **Source:** https://game-icons.net

### KayKit (Kay Lousberg): 3D characters, animations, weapons, buildings and nature

- **What:** the models and animations embedded in `js/models3d.js` and `js/m3d/*.js`, built by `tools/build3d.js` from: KayKit Character Pack Adventures 1.0, KayKit Adventurers 2.0 (Knight, Ranger, Mage), KayKit Skeletons 1.0, KayKit Character Animations 1.1, KayKit Fantasy Weapons Bits 1.0, KayKit Medieval Hexagon Pack 1.0 and KayKit Forest Nature. The reference image `art/kaykit2/heroes.jpg` is from the Adventurers 2.0 pack.
- **Village growth (9.11.2026):** the ruin, building stages, tower base, fences and props (`vx_*` in `js/m3d/`) are also from KayKit Medieval Hexagon Pack 1.0 (`tools/build3d.js --only village`).
- **License:** [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain dedication). No attribution required; given anyway.
- **Source:** https://kaylousberg.itch.io/ and https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets. Details: [docs/3d-assets.md](docs/3d-assets.md).

### Quaternius: arena monsters and the pet skeleton

- **What:** Ultimate Monsters (the `mon_*` models and their animations in `js/m3d/`), and the Husky model whose skeleton and animations drive the pets (`dog_*` clips; the pet meshes themselves are ours, see "Made for this game").
- **License:** [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **Source:** https://quaternius.com and https://poly.pizza/m/wcWiuEqwzq

### Creative Claw: painted UI art and icons

- **What:** everything under `icons/ui/`: the title image, wooden frame, buttons, navigation icons, the daily chest, item images (`gear/`), resource icons (`res/`), NPC portraits (`npc/`) and the painted skill icons (`skills/`). Probably also the app icons in `icons/` (`icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `maskable-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`), made in the same batch; **to verify**.
- **License:** generated for this game on the Creative Claw service with explicit permission from its creator, Itay ("use the images freely", 31 October 2026). The images are the game's own; the underlying generators are OpenAI gpt-image and Google Nano Banana models, whose terms allow use of generated output. Details: [docs/creative-claw.md](docs/creative-claw.md).

### Kenney: sound effects

- **What:** 22 of the 32 files in `sounds/` (embedded as `js/sounds-data.js`), converted to mono 22 kHz MP3 and trimmed. The Kenney packs: Impact, Interface, RPG Audio, Digital Audio, Sci-fi and Casino Audio. The per-file mapping to the original Kenney packs (Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds, RPG Audio, Digital Audio) is in [sounds/README.md](sounds/README.md). `bell.mp3` and `creak.mp3` were synthesized in code and are ours.
- **License:** [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **Source:** https://kenney.nl

### lentikula, CoMiGo, stumpystrust and rubberduck: spell and event sounds

- **What:** `cast_fire.mp3`, `cast_water.mp3`, `cast_ice.mp3` (lentikula, Basic Spell Impacts), `cast_earth.mp3` (lentikula, Druid Spell Impacts), `me_back.mp3` (lentikula, Healing Spell Impacts) and `cast_lightning.mp3` (CoMiGo, Bleeps and Bloops: ElectroZap), via the OpenGameArt collection "150 combat sound effects"; `wave_start.mp3` (stumpystrust, "Their coming" generic horn) and `ult_ready.mp3` (rubberduck, 80 CC0 RPG SFX), from OpenGameArt. Converted to MP3 and trimmed. Per-file mapping in [sounds/README.md](sounds/README.md).
- **License:** [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **Source:** https://opengameart.org

### Fonts: Heebo and Secular One

- **What:** loaded at runtime from Google Fonts (`index.html`); not redistributed in the repository. Heebo by Oded Ezer, Secular One by Ben Nathan.
- **License:** [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/).

### three.js and bundled helpers

- **What:** `js/vendor/three-bundle.js`: three.js r186 with GLTFLoader, SkeletonUtils and BufferGeometryUtils (all part of the three.js project), plus MeshoptDecoder from meshoptimizer (Arseny Kapoulkine). Built by `tools/build3d.js`.
- **License:** MIT (three.js: Copyright 2010-2026 Three.js Authors; meshoptimizer: Copyright 2016-2026 Arseny Kapoulkine). Requires keeping the copyright notice, which the bundle header does.
- **Source:** https://threejs.org and https://github.com/zeux/meshoptimizer

### Development tools (not shipped with the game)

- **Playwright** (Apache-2.0) runs the automated test (`tools/check.js`). **esbuild**, **gltf-transform**, **sharp**, **meshoptimizer** (MIT / Apache-2.0) build the 3D bundle. **Blender** (GPL, outputs are ours) built two weapons in `tools/blender/`. None of these ship in the game.
- **Services:** hosting on Vercel, cloud saves on Supabase (the game talks to its REST API with its own code; no Supabase client library is bundled). Images for item models were generated on Hugging Face Spaces (below).

## Made for this game

These are the project's own work and need no external credit; listed so nobody looks for a license.

- **Hero and pet drawings** (`art/heroes/*.jpg`, `art/pets/*.jpg`, `art/pilot/*.jpg`): drawn by Nir with Google Gemini image generation, in KayKit's style.
- **Hero, pet and weapon 3D models** (`art/heroes/*.glb`, `art/pets/*.glb`, `art/pilot/*.glb`, `art/weapons/raw/*.glb`): built from those drawings and from the Creative Claw item images with TRELLIS (Microsoft, MIT) and Hunyuan3D 2 (Tencent) on Hugging Face Spaces, then re-rigged onto the KayKit or Quaternius skeleton. The generated meshes belong to the project. **To verify:** the Tencent Hunyuan community license has conditions for very large commercial deployments; irrelevant for a private game, but worth one look if the game is ever published.
- **Two Blender weapons** (`starStaff`, `skyBow`): modelled from code in `tools/blender/`.
- **Everything else:** code, 2D canvas drawing, design, texts and the two synthesized sounds.
